The challenge

Japan is running short of engineers.

According to estimates from Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI), Japan could face a significant shortage of IT professionals by 2030. Manufacturing and engineering companies face the same recruitment pressure.

Job-O-Hire connects that hiring need with skilled Indian students and recent graduates — engineers who want to work in Japan but lack the right platform or connection.

Who we are

An India–Japan talent connection.

Founded by Rupesh Jahagirdar, a Master's student in Engineering at IIT Dhanbad. Job-O-Hire connects Indian students and graduates with internship and full-time opportunities at Japanese companies.

"Talent in India lives in every street and small town, but many talented students never get a chance to apply and reach global companies. Japan needs skilled engineers, and many Indian students want to work there — but they don't have the right platform or connection. Job-O-Hire is my way to build that bridge."

Our purpose: help companies find suitable people and help young talent access opportunities beyond their location or reach.
